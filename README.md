SMOKE & THUNDER — a judged aerobatic airshow circuit: fly 18 named figures through judged gate rings across 5 Indonesian venues, trailing coloured smoke that draws each figure across the sky. This v2 rebuilds the renderer as a true chase camera behind a large biplane, with a real 3D gate ring and a visible runway ahead, over recognisable terrain.

Three routines (Precision, Freestyle, Crowd Line), three rival pilots (Elang, Sari, Bimo), three judges, announcer Mbak Rani, and fuel, G-damage and smoke-fluid decisions make every flight a scored performance — with a takeoff and a scored landing at each of the 5 venues: Teluk Arunika, Kawah Merapi, Ngarai Senja, Pelabuhan Senja and Langit Malam.
