SMOKE & THUNDER: fly the Cendrawasih airshow circuit — 5 venues, 3 routines, 18 named figures through judged gate rings, trailing coloured smoke that draws the figure across the sky. Three rival pilots, three judges scoring every figure in-frame, a crowd meter, and an upgrade shop between venues. The tour runs from the lagoon dawn of Teluk Arunika through the volcano noon of Kawah Merapi, the golden-hour Ngarai Senja canyon, the harbour dusk of Pelabuhan Senja, and the night finale over Langit Malam.
