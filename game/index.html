How to fly

W/S or ↑/↓ pitch  •  A/D or ←/→ bank & turn  •  Shift throttle up / Ctrl throttle down  •  Space smoke on/off  •  P pause  •  M mute

Fly through the gold rings in order. Keep the speed near the figure's target, keep the smoke on, and don't pull more G than the airframe likes. Land on the runway strip to finish — a gentle touchdown is worth points.
